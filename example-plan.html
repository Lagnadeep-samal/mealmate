Recipes

Monday breakfast, 15 min

Banana rice porridge

  • 1 cup short grain rice
  • 2 banana
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  1. Simmer the rice in 3 cups water for 20 minutes.
  2. Stir in mashed banana, cinnamon and syrup.
Monday lunch, 30 min

Chickpea and tomato stew

  • 2 cups chickpeas, cooked
  • 4 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 handfuls baby spinach
  1. Soften onion and garlic in oil.
  2. Add cumin, tomatoes and chickpeas; simmer 20 minutes.
  3. Wilt in the spinach.
Monday dinner, 40 min

Lemon herb chicken with roast potatoes

  • 600 g chicken thighs
  • 600 g potatoes
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • 250 g green beans
  1. Toss everything but the beans in oil and lemon.
  2. Roast at 220C for 35 minutes.
  3. Steam the beans and serve alongside.
Tuesday breakfast, 20 min

Potato and pepper hash

  • 400 g potatoes
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  1. Dice and par-boil the potatoes.
  2. Fry with onion and pepper until crisp, then add paprika.
Tuesday lunch, 30 min

Red lentil soup

  • 1.5 cups red lentils
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 litre vegetable stock
  1. Sweat onion and carrot.
  2. Add lentils, cumin and stock; simmer 20 minutes and blend.
Tuesday dinner, 25 min

Beef and broccoli rice bowls

  • 400 g beef strips
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 1.5 cups short grain rice
  • 1 thumb fresh ginger
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 lime
  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Stir-fry beef with ginger and garlic, add broccoli, finish with lime.
Wednesday breakfast, 20 min

Sweet potato and avocado plate

  • 2 sweet potato
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  1. Slice and pan-fry the sweet potato.
  2. Top with avocado and a squeeze of lime.
Wednesday lunch, 20 min

Chicken and rice salad

  • 300 g cooked chicken
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cucumber
  • 200 g cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Chop everything and toss with lemon juice and oil.
Wednesday dinner, 35 min

Turkey meatballs with zucchini

  • 500 g ground turkey
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bunch fresh parsley
  • 3 zucchini
  • 4 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. Mix turkey, grated onion and parsley; roll into balls.
  2. Brown, then simmer in tomatoes for 15 minutes.
  3. Serve over sauteed zucchini.
